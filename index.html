IPScan

A fast, native macOS network scanner.

Support

Questions or bug reports: open an issue.

Privacy Policy

IPScan does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information. Scan results and device nicknames are stored only on your Mac in the app's local preferences. IPScan performs local-network operations (ARP, TCP, Bonjour/mDNS) only to discover devices on the network you are connected to; nothing leaves your local network.
